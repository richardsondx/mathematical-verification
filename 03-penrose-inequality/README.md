# 03 Penrose inequality

Status: map only, nothing proved, Nov 25, 2026.

## Question

Does the mass of an asymptotically flat spacetime bound the area of its horizons, in the form Penrose proposed?

## Answer so far

- No proof in this repo.
- The map lists what is proved and what stays open.
- Map: PENROSE-MAP.md
- Gaps: PENROSE-REPORT.md

## What is proved

- Riemannian case, 3D, largest connected horizon: Huisken and Ilmanen, 2001.
- Riemannian case, 3D, total area: Bray, 2001.
- Riemannian case, dimensions below 8: Bray and Lee, 2009.
- Spherical symmetry: Malec and O Murchadha, 1994.
- Charged case, connected horizon: Jang and Wald argument, weak flow 2001.

## What stays open

- Full Lorentzian case, general data.
- Riemannian case in dimension 8 and above.
- General AdS case.
- Penrose inequality with angular momentum in full generality.
